Which way is up

Turn each object until its top faces you, and press the button

O‑Voxel columnPaper
  1. Pick an object.
  2. Drag to turn it. Left and right spins it; up and down tips it over, all the way to looking straight down on it.
  3. When the object's top is facing you, press this face is the top.
  4. Turn it over and press this face is the bottom.

Nothing has to be named. The direction is worked out from where you were looking when you pressed, and the answer appears at the bottom to send back. The two are asked for separately rather than one being assumed opposite the other, because a check that agrees is worth having.

Left and right are not asked for: these objects are bodies of revolution about one axis, so once the top is known there is nothing left to pin down. The arrows on the object are the lattice's own axes — red +x, green +y, blue +z — and they are there to read, not to choose from.
